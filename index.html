Weather Rock privacy policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026 · Operated by ReliableITStudio · Privacy contact: diegoeg@reliableits.net

Weather Rock shows current weather through an animated hanging rock. You can search for a city, or you can optionally allow Android's approximate foreground location permission. We do not request background or precise location permission, and city search works without location access.

Weather requests

When you search for a city, the app sends your search text and chosen language (English, Spanish, Portuguese or Hindi) to Open-Meteo's geocoding service. When you request weather, the app sends the coordinates of your chosen city, or of your approximate device location, to Open-Meteo over HTTPS, both to its forecast service and to its air-quality service (air-quality data comes from the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service). The service also receives connection information such as your IP address. These requests are what make city search, weather and air quality work.

For its free API, Open-Meteo states that troubleshooting logs may include geographic coordinates and are deleted after 90 days. Its policy also describes IP processing for technical and abuse-prevention purposes. See open-meteo.com/en/terms for the provider's current terms and privacy details.

To catch rain that forecasts miss, the app also asks the Aviation Weather Center of the US National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) for recent airport weather reports in an area of roughly 90 by 90 km around your chosen place, over HTTPS. The app rounds the coordinates before this request, so NOAA receives that area, not your exact position, plus connection information such as your IP address. NOAA is a US government agency; see weather.gov/privacy for its privacy policy.

Information on your device

The app keeps these settings in its private local storage: your selected place name and coordinates, whether that place came from your device location, your temperature unit, language and appearance setting. The latest report is held only in app memory. Android backup is disabled. To remove the stored preferences, clear Weather Rock's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. If you revoke location permission, the app stops making device-location requests, and you can still use manual city search.

This version has no Weather Rock account, no developer-operated backend, no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no cloud profile. Weather Rock does not sell your data. The app's requests to the weather providers are still a transfer of data off your phone.

Children

Weather Rock is not designed to collect personal information from children and has no accounts or profiles.

Contacts and changes

If you email support, the mailbox provider and the operator receive whatever information you choose to include. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to answer your request and are then deleted, unless the law requires otherwise. For privacy questions or data requests, contact diegoeg@reliableits.net. Deleting data on your device does not delete the provider's server logs. If we change providers or add advertising or purchases, we will update this policy and provide any required disclosures or consent.
